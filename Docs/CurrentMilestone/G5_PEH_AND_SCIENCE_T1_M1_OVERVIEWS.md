# Grade 5 PE & Health and Science Term 1 Mission 1 — Three-Area Overviews

These missions validate that the reusable runtime supports subject-specific actions without copying the LiteraQuest learning activity.

## PE & Health — The Storm Inside

Primary competency: recognize stress signals and apply healthy coping strategies.

Mechanic family: observe situation → choose safe response → perform action → see wellness result.

- Area 1 Discover — Signal Garden: identify relevant stress clues, confirm that clues are not a diagnosis, perform paced breathing, restore the garden, collect Wellness Symbol 1.
- Area 2 Apply — Plan Path: choose one manageable step, identify a trusted helper, practice a respectful request, restore the support route, collect Wellness Symbol 2.
- Area 3 Master — Rest Grove: assemble a balanced coping plan, identify when trusted-adult support is needed, apply the plan, restore the park, collect Wellness Symbol 3, complete the mission.

Each area has one principal health situation, one predefined in-world healthy action, and three scored checks. There is no separate reminder, healthy-choice completion, area-review, or learning-summary modal.

## Science — The Vanishing Supply Cart

Primary competency: matter has mass and occupies space; solids, liquids, and gases are forms of matter.

Mechanic family: observe → predict → investigate → record → conclude → apply.

- Area 1 Discover — Solid and Liquid Depot: predict and observe a fixed amount of water changing container shape, record one result, classify evidence, restore the station, collect Evidence Token 1.
- Area 2 Apply — Gas and Mass Yard: predict balloon expansion, add air, observe space/mass evidence, record one result, restore the station, collect Evidence Token 2.
- Area 3 Master — Classification Gate: classify the block, bottled water, and air-filled ball through one integrated sorting interaction, confirm the shared properties of matter, restore the cart, collect Evidence Token 3, complete the mission.

Areas 1 and 2 use one unscored prediction plus three scored checks. Area 3 uses three scored mastery checks. Material pickup is automatic after interaction; no complex inventory or drag-and-drop is required.

Exact dialogue, questions, answer keys, hints, actions, and results are in the mission JSON files. The current milestone implements the shared loaders/runtime and LiteraQuest M1 vertical slice first; PE & Health and Science scenes remain later milestones.
