# Science — The Vanishing Supply Cart

```text
g5_sci_t1_m01
Solid and Liquid Depot → Gas and Mass Yard → Classification Gate
three Science Evidence Tokens
```

- Area 1: compare a solid and a fixed amount of liquid in different containers.
- Area 2: use an inflated sealed balloon and guided balance evidence to show gas occupies space and matter has mass.
- Area 3: classify a block, bottled water, and air-filled ball; explain that all have mass and occupy space; restore the cart.

The defining subject action is: observe, record an unscored prediction, investigate, record evidence, answer, conclude, and apply.

Exact dialogue, questions, answer keys, hints, actions, and results are in the two mission JSON files. The current milestone implements only their loaders/data foundations and overview validation, not the scenes.
